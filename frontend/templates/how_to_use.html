Step-by-Step Guides

Analyst Workflows

Detailed, module-specific operational procedures for the most common analyst tasks.

01
Navigate to the Login Portal
Open the CyberBuddy SOC platform in your browser. You will arrive at the main authentication screen. The animated world map in the background is the live geo-intelligence canvas — it will later reflect real threat data.
Always access the platform over HTTPS. The system enforces TLS 1.3 and rate-limits login attempts.
02
Enter Your Analyst Credentials
Enter your Operator ID (username) and Passphrase in the authentication card, then click Login to SOC Platform. If you do not have an account, click Request Access to register.
CSRF protection is active on every form. Do not attempt to submit forms programmatically without the token.
03
Explore the Dashboard
After authentication you will land on the SOC Dashboard. The left navigation panel lists all six detection modules. Use the top search bar to find previous scans, or click New Analysis to submit a fresh indicator.
04
Explore Modules (No Login Required)
On the login screen, click Explore Modules to open the full-screen module overview without logging in. This is designed for demonstrations and onboarding new analysts.
01
Select URL Scanner
From the dashboard, click URL Scanner in the sidebar or the module card on the home screen.
02
Paste the Suspicious URL
Paste the full URL into the input field — including the protocol (http:// or https://). The scanner handles raw URLs, shortened links, and redirect chains.
https://suspicious-login.example-bank.com/verify?token=abc123
03
Review Feature Extraction Output
The scanner extracts 20+ features including domain age, IP reputation, URL length, special character density, subdomain depth, HTTPS validity, redirect count, and lexical entropy. All extracted features are shown in the results panel.
04
Read the ML Verdict + OSINT Enrichment
The Random Forest model outputs a threat probability score (0–100%). Alongside the ML result, OSINT lookups retrieve reputation data from threat intelligence feeds and display geolocation, hosting provider, and blacklist status.
A score above 70% is considered High Risk. Scores between 40–70% indicate Suspicious. Always cross-reference with OSINT data.
05
Forward to AI Analyst
Click Analyze with AI to pass the scan results to the AI Analyst for a threat narrative, MITRE ATT&CK technique identification, and remediation guidance.
01
Select Email Analysis
Navigate to Email Analysis from the dashboard sidebar.
02
Paste the Raw Email Body
Copy and paste the full text body of the suspicious email into the text area. Include the full content — subject hints, greeting, body, footer, and any visible URLs. Do not include raw email headers unless instructed.
The NLP model was trained on diverse phishing corpora. Best results require at least 3–4 sentences of email body text.
03
Review Tokenization and Classification
The NLP pipeline tokenizes the input, removes stop words, applies TF-IDF vectorization, and classifies using a trained scikit-learn model. The output shows which tokens contributed highest weight to the verdict (phishing-indicative n-grams are highlighted).
04
Extract and Scan Embedded URLs
Any URLs found in the email body are automatically extracted and can be forwarded directly to the URL Scanner with a single click — without manually copying them.
01
Choose Screenshot OCR or QR Code Scanner
Use Screenshot OCR for images containing text — phishing pages, chat screenshots, suspicious ads. Use QR Code Scanner when you have a physical or digital QR code to decode.
02
Upload Your Image File
Click the upload area or drag-and-drop your image. Supported formats: PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP, BMP. Maximum file size: 10 MB. The image is processed locally and never transmitted externally.
For best OCR accuracy, use images with a minimum resolution of 800×600 px and clear, high-contrast text.
03
Review Extracted Text / Decoded Payload
OCR: RapidOCR extracts all visible text from the image. Review the extracted text for indicators — URLs, email addresses, phone numbers, brand names.

QR: OpenCV decodes the QR payload (typically a URL). The decoded URL is displayed before any further analysis.
04
Route Indicators to the Appropriate Module
Extracted URLs are automatically routed to the URL Scanner. Email-like content is routed to Email Analysis. All routing can be reviewed and overridden before submission.
01
Complete a Full Analysis Session
Before generating a report, ensure you have completed at least one scan and reviewed the AI Analyst output. Reports are compiled from the current session's analysis history.
02
Open the Report Generator
Click Generate Report at the bottom of any completed analysis page, or navigate directly to Report Generator from the sidebar.
03
Review the Compiled Report
The report includes: original indicator, scan timestamp, analyst ID, extracted features, ML probability score, OSINT findings, AI Analyst narrative, MITRE ATT&CK technique references, risk severity rating, and recommended actions.
04
Download or Share
Download the report as a structured document for filing as an incident record, sharing with your team, or submitting for compliance review.
Reports contain sensitive threat data. Distribute only over secure, authenticated channels within your organization.
Frequently Asked Questions

Common Analyst Questions

Everything you need to know before your first analysis session.

All ML classification and NLP analysis runs entirely on your local server — no indicator is forwarded to external AI endpoints for inference. OSINT lookups (reputation checks, DNS, WHOIS) do make outbound requests to configured intelligence APIs, but these are always optional and configurable in the environment settings.
The URL Scanner's Random Forest model is trained and cross-validated on labeled phishing and benign URL datasets. Accuracy figures vary by dataset composition, but in testing the model achieves above 95% on common phishing patterns. The OSINT enrichment layer provides a second validation signal to reduce false positives. Always treat the ML score as one input, not a final verdict, especially for novel or obfuscated domains.
Yes. All ML and NLP classification modules operate fully offline without any API keys. OSINT enrichment (VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB, OTX, IPInfo, URLhaus) requires keys configured in the .env file. If keys are absent, OSINT results will show as unavailable but all local analysis will function normally.
The OCR module accepts PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP, and BMP formats up to 10 MB. For best results, provide images with high contrast, minimum 800×600 resolution, and horizontal text orientation. Curved, rotated, or highly stylized fonts may reduce OCR confidence.
On the login screen, click Request Access below the login form. Complete the registration form with your Operator ID, email address, and passphrase. New accounts may require administrator approval before access is granted, depending on your platform configuration.
Analysis history and generated reports are stored in the local SQLite database and are accessible across sessions when you log in with the same analyst account. CSRF tokens and session cookies are rotated on each login for security.
