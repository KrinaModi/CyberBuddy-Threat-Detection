Detection Modules

Six Specialized Analysis Engines

Every module is purpose-built for a specific indicator type, with its own feature extraction pipeline, classification engine, and OSINT integration layer.

URL Scanner
Random Forest · OSINT · DNS
Extracts 20+ structural, lexical, and behavioral URL features and classifies them using a trained Random Forest model, cross-validated against real-time OSINT lookups for threat reputation enrichment.
Email Analysis
NLP · TF-IDF · scikit-learn
Applies NLTK tokenization and TF-IDF vectorization to raw email body text, then classifies using a trained NLP model to surface phishing language, social engineering patterns, and urgency manipulation tactics.
Screenshot OCR
RapidOCR · Computer Vision
Processes uploaded images with RapidOCR to extract embedded text — URLs, addresses, brand impersonation copy — then routes extracted indicators through the appropriate classifier pipeline.
QR Code Scanner
OpenCV · Quishing Detection
Decodes QR codes using OpenCV to extract embedded payloads — typically phishing URLs — and immediately subjects them to the full URL Scanner pipeline. Defends against QR-based phishing (quishing).
AI Analyst
LLM · MITRE ATT&CK · Reasoning
Takes structured scan output and generates a human-readable threat narrative with MITRE ATT&CK technique mapping, attacker intent analysis, risk severity rating, and recommended analyst actions.
Report Generator
Structured Output · Compliance
Compiles a complete incident report from any analysis session — original indicator, timestamps, ML scores, OSINT findings, and the AI Analyst narrative — formatted for team sharing and compliance records.

Security Philosophy

Four Core Principles

CyberBuddy is designed around the same principles that define enterprise-grade security tooling: transparency, local processing, and zero assumptions about indicator safety.

No External Telemetry
All ML inference and NLP classification runs locally on your server. Indicators submitted by analysts are never forwarded to third-party telemetry endpoints. Your threat data stays in your environment.
Zero Trust Input
Every indicator submitted — regardless of source — is treated as potentially malicious until the analysis pipeline produces a verdict. No indicator is trusted by default, even from internal sources.
Transparent Scoring
Risk scores are never black boxes. Feature contributions, OSINT findings, and classifier confidence levels are surfaced in the output, so analysts can understand and challenge every verdict.
Defense in Depth
No single engine makes the final call. ML classification, OSINT reputation lookups, NLP analysis, and AI reasoning all operate independently — each cross-validating the others' findings.
