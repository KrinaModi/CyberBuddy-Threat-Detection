URL Scanner
ML + OSINT
Multi-layer phishing and malware URL detection
Submits any URL through a dual-engine pipeline combining a trained Random Forest classifier (20+ extracted features) with real-time OSINT lookups. Detects phishing pages, drive-by malware, domain spoofing, and credential harvesting infrastructure.
Paste URL Feature Extract ML Score OSINT Check Verdict
Email Analysis
NLP Engine
NLP-driven phishing email body classification
Paste the raw body text of any suspicious email. The NLP pipeline tokenizes, vectorizes with TF-IDF, and classifies using a trained scikit-learn model. Identifies social engineering patterns, urgency manipulation, impersonation tactics, and payload delivery language.
Paste Email NLP Tokenize TF-IDF Score Classify Risk Rating
Screenshot OCR
Vision + OCR
Extract and analyze indicators from images
Upload any screenshot — phishing pages, suspicious chat messages, fake login portals. RapidOCR extracts all embedded text, which is then routed through the NLP classifier for threat assessment. Surfaces URLs, email addresses, and social engineering content embedded in images.
Upload Image OCR Extract Indicator Parse NLP Classify
QR Code Scanner
Decode + Verify
Decode and validate QR-encoded threats
QR phishing ("quishing") is a growing attack vector that bypasses traditional email filters. Upload or capture any QR code image. OpenCV decodes the payload — typically a URL — which is immediately routed through the full URL Scanner pipeline for threat assessment.
Upload QR CV Decode URL Extract Full URL Scan
AI Analyst
LLM Reasoning
Natural-language threat explanation and MITRE mapping
The AI Analyst takes structured scan results and generates a human-readable threat narrative: attack category, attacker intent, MITRE ATT&CK technique references, recommended mitigations, and an overall risk rating. Designed to bridge the gap between raw indicator data and actionable SOC intelligence.
Scan Results LLM Context MITRE Map Narrative
Report Generator
Incident Report
Downloadable professional incident reports
Generates a structured, printable incident report from any completed analysis session. Contains the original indicator, scan timestamp, all ML and OSINT findings, the AI Analyst narrative, and a risk summary. Suitable for team sharing, client delivery, and compliance documentation.
